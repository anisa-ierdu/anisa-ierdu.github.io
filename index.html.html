01 — Introduction

About Me

A short introduction to who I am and how I teach.

Hello! I'm a trainee teacher at the Institute of Education and Research, University of Dhaka, focused on early childhood education and inclusive classrooms.

I love turning difficult ideas into simple, playful lessons — using stories, visuals and hands-on activities that help every student feel confident. My teaching approach is calm, structured and built around the child.

Right now I'm looking for opportunities to teach, design learning materials, and collaborate with schools and educators on projects that make classrooms kinder and more effective.

Frequently Asked

The questions people usually ask before hiring or collaborating.

What kind of work are you looking for?

Teaching and teaching-support roles in primary or early-years settings, plus freelance curriculum design, lesson planning and learning-material design.

Are you available for part-time or remote work?

Yes. I'm currently studying, so part-time, weekend, remote and short-term project work all fit well around my schedule.

Can you design materials for a low-resource classroom?

Absolutely — that's my favourite kind of work. Everything I design can be printed in black and white and made from cheap or recycled materials.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks, depending on the scope of the project. Get in touch and we can work out a timeline together.
