01 — Introduction

About Me

A short introduction to who I am.

Hello! I'm a student at the Institute of Education and Research, University of Dhaka, studying in the Educational Psychology and Guidance department.

I love applying psychology theories to create simple, playful lessons — using stories, visuals and hands-on activities that help every student feel included. My approach is structured and built around the individual child: a habit shaped by my Special Education background, my internship with children with special needs, and my current studies in Educational Psychology & Guidance at the University of Dhaka.

My goal is to make psychological knowledge accessible, usable and genuinely helpful — whether through teacher training, curriculum design, or direct work with children. To that end, I'm looking for opportunities to teach, design lessons, and collaborate with schools and educators on projects that make learning more effective and enjoyable.

Let's make learning more enjoyable together. Anisa Anwar Safia · Student, Educator & Trainer

09 — Good to know

Frequently Asked

The questions people usually ask before hiring or collaborating.

What kind of training do you design?

Two main kinds: modules for teachers on classroom engagement and behaviour, and modules for parents on early childhood development. Both are built on psychology theory but written in plain language, with session plans, facilitator notes and participant handouts included.

Can you run the training, or only design it?

Both. I can hand over a complete, ready-to-deliver module for your team to run, or facilitate the sessions myself — in person in Dhaka or online. I can also adapt the length to fit your schedule.

Do you work with children with special needs?

Yes — this is a core part of my background. I completed my teaching practicum at Dhaka Government Badhir High School, teaching children with hearing impairment, and my B.Ed. specialisation was in Special Education.

Do you take on research or scale adaptation work?

Yes. I've translated and adapted the Brief Resilience Scale into Bangla, working through forward translation, back-translation, expert review and pilot testing. I'm open to similar work in educational psychology.

Are you available for part-time or remote work?

Yes. I'm currently studying, so part-time, weekend, remote and short-term project work all fit well around my schedule.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks, depending on the scope of the project. Get in touch and we can work out a timeline together.
